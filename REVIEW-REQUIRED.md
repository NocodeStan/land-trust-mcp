## Review sign-off

Before publication, obtain written confirmation from:

1. A qualified Lagos property lawyer called to the Nigerian Bar:
   - That all procedural guidance is accurate and current
   - That no content constitutes legal advice
   - That the disclaimer is adequate

2. A SURCON-registered surveyor:
   - That all survey-related guidance is accurate
   - That the description of globally acquired areas is not misleading
